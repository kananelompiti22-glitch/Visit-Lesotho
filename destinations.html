Sani Pass

Sani Pass reaches an altitude of 2,876 meters. It's one of Africa's most spectacular mountain passes!

🚗 Best experienced with a 4x4 vehicle — the winding road offers breathtaking views at every turn.

At the very top, you'll find the highest pub in Africa — a perfect place to rest and enjoy the scenery! 🍺

Thabana Ntlenyana

Thabana Ntlenyana means "Little Beautiful Mountain" in Sesotho. Standing at 3,482m, it's the highest point in Southern Africa!

🥾 It's a popular multi-day hike. The summit rewards you with panoramic views stretching into South Africa and over the Lesotho highlands.

💡 Tip: Go with a guide — the scenery is stunning but the weather changes fast!

Katse Dam

Katse Dam is the second-highest dam in Africa. The blue water against the mountains looks like a painting!

🎣 Activities: Fishing, boat cruises, scenic drives and photography. The sunset here is unforgettable. 🌅

It's also part of the Lesotho Highlands Water Project — truly an engineering marvel!

Maletsunyane Falls

Maletsunyane Falls drops an incredible 192 meters in a single leap — one of Africa's most impressive waterfalls!

💦 The mist can be seen from far away. The sound is powerful and majestic — just like Lesotho itself.

📍 Near the village of Semonkong — meaning "Place of Smoke" — named after the falls! 🏡

Sehlabathebe National Park

Sehlabathebe is Lesotho's very first national park — a UNESCO World Heritage Site candidate!

🦅 Home to beautiful wild flowers, wetlands, birds and even the rare bearded vulture.

Perfect for peaceful hiking. The silence here is beautiful — just you and nature. 🌿

Tshehlanyane Nature Reserve

Tshehlanyane means "Place of Peace" — and that's exactly what you'll find here. 🍃

🌲 The largest protected indigenous forest in Lesotho. Pine trees, mountain streams and fresh air everywhere!

Great hiking trails, horse riding, and comfortable lodges make this perfect for a weekend escape. 🏡
